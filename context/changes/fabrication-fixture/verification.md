## Primary metric and read-off

Per-run **M2+M3 rate** (`fabricationRuns` out of 20), with per-mechanism
sub-counts always recorded. Comparator: the campaign's CI baseline **B = 17**.

**Success bar (frozen, from the archived fixture-spec):** the fixture is
**REPRESENTATIVE** iff both hold at n=20:

1. fabricationRuns within the UNCHANGED band, **|count − 17| ≤ 3** (i.e. 14–20);
2. the mechanism split is **M3-dominant** — M3 is the single largest of
   {M1, M2, M3} by finding count.

Anything outside band 1 → **NOT REPRESENTATIVE**, recorded as such with the
count; the fixture is then a synthetic artifact that does not reproduce the
real diff's behaviour, which is a legitimate and publishable negative.

**Secondary observables (recorded, never gated):** M1 rate (the fixture plants
exactly one M1 target, so M1 > 0 confirms the cap mechanism is live), M2 rate,
and the collapse-signature rate.

## Hand-read protocol (inherited)

Every rubric-flagged finding (mechanism ≠ none) plus **10 deterministic clean
controls** (iterate runs 1..20 in order, taking each gradeable run's
lowest-indexed `none` finding; repeat with the next-lowest until 10 are
collected). Misgrade rate ≥ 15% invalidates the grading — stop, record, no
read-off.
